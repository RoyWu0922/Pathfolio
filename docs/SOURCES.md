## Notes for implementers

- [1]–[3] are competitor UX, not scientific sources. Never put their marketing percentages in Pathfolio UI.
- [10] is the 2012 study (6 seconds, six data points, 80% of gaze). [11] is the 2018 update (7.4 seconds). Say which year you mean.
- [16] “98.9% full employment” is employed / (employed + unemployed), not a placement rate. Destinations table is the honest graphic.
- Schmidt & Hunter (1998) structured-interview validity was **not** independently extracted from the original PDF in this planning pass (the copy we tried timed out). Do not quote r = .51 in the product until someone files the PDF and adds [20].
